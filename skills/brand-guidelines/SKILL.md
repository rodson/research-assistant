# Brand Guidelines

## Typography
- Use Inter for headings and body text.

## Colors
- Primary: #0066CC
- Accent: #F5C26B

## Tone of voice
- Friendly and direct. Short sentences, no jargon.
- Speak to the customer as "you", never "the user".
